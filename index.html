The Story


In a world where light and darkness once existed in harmony, an ancient crystal begins to shatter, threatening the fate of Aetheria. Join Sora, a young knight with an ordinary dream, alongside Eiko, a brilliant mage, and Rin, a gentle healer, as they journey through enchanted forests, frozen mountains, and forgotten kingdoms.

Uncover the truth behind Yami, the feared Demon Lord, and discover that the greatest darkness may hide a forgotten promise. With friendship, courage, and a little hope, even the smallest step can lead to a brighter tomorrow.
